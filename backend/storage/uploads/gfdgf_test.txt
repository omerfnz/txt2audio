Evan loved exploring the old forest behind his grandmother’s house. One misty morning, he stumbled upon a tiny wooden door hidden at the base of a giant oak tree. Curious, he knocked gently — and to his surprise, the door slowly swung open by itself.

Inside, a warm golden light spilled out, revealing a small room filled with glowing mushrooms and floating dust that sparkled like stars. A soft whisper called his name, inviting him deeper. Evan took a step inside, knowing that his ordinary day had just turned into an extraordinary adventure.
